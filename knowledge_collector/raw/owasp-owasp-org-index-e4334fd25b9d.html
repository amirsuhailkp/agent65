It’s our 25th Anniversary! Get ready for an unforgettable experience at the OWASP Global AppSec EU Conference!

From June 22-26, 2026, join over 800 cybersecurity experts at the legendary Austria Center in Vienna, Austria for an event like no other. This year’s reimagined conference promises to ignite your passion for security with world class keynotes, newly designed tracks , OWASP Project Demo’s, interactive PODS, and MobileAppSecCon.
